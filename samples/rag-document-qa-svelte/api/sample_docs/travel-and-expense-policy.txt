# Contoso Travel and Expense Policy

## Booking travel
All business travel is booked through Navan, the Contoso travel portal, so that trips are covered by our duty-of-care program. Flights must be booked at least 14 days in advance when possible. Economy class is required for flights under six hours; premium economy is allowed for flights of six hours or longer.

## Hotels
Hotel stays should not exceed the nightly city cap shown in Navan, which is $250 for most US cities and $325 for New York, San Francisco, London, and Tokyo. Stays above the cap require pre-approval from your manager.

## Meals and per diem
While traveling, meals are reimbursed up to a daily limit of $75, including tips. Alcohol is not reimbursable unless it is part of an approved client entertainment event. Meals with colleagues who are not traveling are not reimbursed.

## Submitting expenses
Expenses are submitted in Concur within 30 days of the purchase. An itemized receipt is required for every expense of $25 or more. Approved expense reports are reimbursed with the next payroll run, typically within 10 business days.

## Corporate card
Employees who travel more than four times per year can request a Contoso corporate card. The corporate card may only be used for business expenses, and personal charges must be repaid within 15 days.

## Mileage
Use of a personal vehicle for business trips is reimbursed at the current IRS standard mileage rate. Commuting between your home and your regular office is not reimbursable.
